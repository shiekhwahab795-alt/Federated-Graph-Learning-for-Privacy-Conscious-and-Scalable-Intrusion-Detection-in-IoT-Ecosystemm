# Federated-Graph-Learning-for-Privacy-Conscious-and-Scalable-Intrusion-Detection-in-IoT-Ecosystemm
This repository introduces a novel framework titled Federated Graph Learning for Privacy-Conscious and Scalable Intrusion Detection in IoT Ecosystems. Drawing on over two decades of research in artificial intelligence, distributed systems, and cybersecurity, the work reflects a mature perspective on the pressing challenges of safeguarding Internet of Things (IoT) infrastructures against increasingly sophisticated threats.

The proposed approach integrates federated learning with graph-based modeling to achieve two critical objectives: preserving privacy and ensuring scalability. Unlike traditional centralized intrusion detection systems, which often expose sensitive data and struggle with heterogeneity across devices, this federated graph model enables collaborative detection without requiring raw data exchange. Each IoT node contributes to the collective intelligence by sharing model updates, thereby maintaining confidentiality while enhancing detection accuracy across diverse environments.

Graph structures are employed to capture the intricate relationships among IoT devices, communication flows, and potential attack vectors. This representation allows the system to detect subtle anomalies and coordinated intrusions that may remain hidden in isolated analyses. To further optimize performance, the framework incorporates feature selection techniques, ensuring that intrusion detection remains computationally feasible even for resource-constrained IoT devices.

Beyond technical innovation, the repository emphasizes reproducibility and transparency. It provides modular code, detailed documentation, and experimental results that validate the effectiveness of the approach across multiple intrusion scenarios. Researchers and practitioners can adapt the framework to domains ranging from smart homes and healthcare systems to industrial IoT networks.

Ultimately, this repository contributes to advancing secure, scalable, and privacy-conscious intrusion detection solutions for IoT ecosystems. It is designed as both a research foundation and a practical toolkit, enabling the community to strengthen defenses against evolving cyber threats while respecting the privacy of distributed data sources.
